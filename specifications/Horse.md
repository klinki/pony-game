# Horse Specification

A Horse is the central entity in the Pony game. Players take care of horses and race with them.

## Properties

Each horse has the following properties. All property values are integers ranging from 0 to 100.

- **Stamina**: Represents the horse's endurance. Affects how long the horse can maintain top speed in a race.
- **Speed**: Represents the horse's maximum velocity.
- **Strength**: Represents the horse's physical power. May affect performance in difficult terrain or obstacles.
- **Beauty**: Represents the horse's aesthetic appeal. Important for competitions or shows (potential future feature) or general value.

## Constraints

- All properties must be integers.
- Minimum value: 0
- Maximum value: 100
